## Floating lyrics

Branch `m7/floating-lyrics` (from `next/windows` aa62484). Settings › Lyric style's "Floating lyrics" row is live: the
lyrics show either on the wallpaper or in a see-through window over every app, never both, each with its own style.
Behaviour and settings: docs/APP-SHELL.md, "Floating lyrics (milestone 7)"; keys in its settings table.

### The window's design

- One more surface on the engine's render thread, in a window owned by its own thread (`FloatingWindowHost`), handed
  over through `IFloatingWindowSink` as the desktop host hands over the wallpapers. Reusing the engine gives it the
  GPU per monitor, the composition swap chain, pacing, power caps and device-loss recovery for free; it survives
  wallpaper rebuilds and is left out of the wallpaper status (so it never keeps system audio running).
- Never focus: no activation anywhere (styles, `SWP_NOACTIVATE`, `MA_NOACTIVATE`, `SW_SHOWNOACTIVATE`-style showing),
  edit mode clears only `WS_EX_TRANSPARENT` and drags with `SetCapture`, never `HTCAPTION`'s system move loop.
- Over full-screen apps: `HWND_TOPMOST`, re-asserted on `EVENT_SYSTEM_FOREGROUND` and every 2 s while shown.
- Every virtual desktop: a tool window with no owner isn't tracked by the virtual desktop manager, so it shows on
  every desktop. Expected, not seen (switching desktops would disturb the owner's session).
- Displays: keyed by DisplayConfig's monitor device path (stable across restarts and port), named by its friendly
  name; a chosen display that isn't connected falls back to the main one. Auto-hidden taskbars are reserved on their
  edge (the work area doesn't leave them out).

  (\\.\DISPLAY1, Intel GPU, 120 dpi) and its surface drawing. A read-only probe (`GetWindowLongPtr`, `WindowFromPoint`,
  `GetForegroundWindow`) read style `0x94000000` (`WS_POPUP | WS_VISIBLE | WS_CLIPSIBLINGS`), extended style

- **Displays off:** in later runs the displays were off (`DisplayOff`): the window stayed shown (`WS_VISIBLE`) while its

- **Settings › Lyric style** (`--settings-page floating --quiet-windows --theme light|dark`): a dark capture while the
  displays were on shows "Show lyrics" with "Floating over apps" chosen, the Floating style caption and the Size row

- **WER / Event Viewer:** no Lunalyr.exe fault or hang during these runs (the only entries were another agent's

### Not seen live

- The window's pixels on a real screen (DirectComposition through a layered, transparent window): only the back
  buffer and the offscreen previews were seen. Clicks passing through were checked with `WindowFromPoint`, not with a
  real click.
- Edit position's drag, resize and Done with a real mouse, and the cursor shapes: never turned on in a live run, so a
  click-taking topmost window never sat over the owner's work.
- Staying above a real full-screen app or game, other virtual desktops, a monitor being unplugged or the window moving
  between the iGPU's and the NVIDIA GPU's displays, auto-hidden taskbars, and the light settings capture of the section.
