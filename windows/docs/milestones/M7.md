### The window's design

- One more surface on the engine's render thread, in a window owned by its own thread (`FloatingWindowHost`), handed
  over through `IFloatingWindowSink` as the desktop host hands over the wallpapers. Reusing the engine gives it the
  GPU per monitor, the composition swap chain, pacing, power caps and device-loss recovery for free; it survives
  wallpaper rebuilds and is left out of the wallpaper status (so it never keeps system audio running).
- Never focus: no activation anywhere (styles, `SWP_NOACTIVATE`, `MA_NOACTIVATE`, `SW_SHOWNOACTIVATE`-style showing),
  edit mode clears only `WS_EX_TRANSPARENT` and drags with `SetCapture`, never `HTCAPTION`'s system move loop.
- Over full-screen apps: `HWND_TOPMOST`, re-asserted on `EVENT_SYSTEM_FOREGROUND` and every 2 s while shown.
- Every virtual desktop: a tool window with no owner isn't tracked by the virtual desktop manager, so it shows on
  every desktop. Expected, not seen (switching desktops would disturb the owner's session).
- Displays: keyed by DisplayConfig's monitor device path (stable across restarts and port), named by its friendly
  name; a chosen display that isn't connected falls back to the main one. Auto-hidden taskbars are reserved on their
  edge (the work area doesn't leave them out).

**Windows' own decisions:**
- **The song is recorded passively.** The Mac seeks the player to just before the lines and plays them while it records;
  the Windows brief says never play anything, so Lunalyr sends no command for it: "Get the song's sound" waits (up to two
  minutes) for the person to play the song from a moment before the lines, records loopback while they play, and stops if
  the song pauses or jumps. Recording is off under `--demo` and `--no-playback`. Open question for the owner: seek and play
  through `PlaybackGuard` as the Mac does?
- The Mac first reads the song's own file through AppleScript when Music has one; Apple Music for Windows exposes no file,
  so Windows always records.
- **Save… picks a folder** (the Windows App SDK's `FolderPicker`, as the Mac's NSOpenPanel does), since several formats are
  made at once; the files are numbered so nothing is overwritten.
- Timed lines are drawn by the wallpaper's own composition layers (the Mac's card uses its `LyricLineView`): the card's type
  size is given to the layer as the floating lyrics' fixed text size, the layout fits it to the stage, and the text size
  slider scales the stage. The layer runs its own lyric ink (drifting by song time), not the Mac's per-frame
  `LyricInkDirector` driven by the voice.
- While the card holds its last moment, the time drawn stops just short of the line's end, so a layout keeps every word
  sung and the line on the stage (the Mac's phrase timing with no next line).
- The GIF is Bayer-dithered against each frame's own palette and, after its first frame, holds only what changed (see
  Checked); the Mac's ImageIO encoder does its own palette work.
- MP4 sound: the Mac writes an M4A and copies both tracks with `AVAssetExportSession`; Windows encodes AAC straight into a
  new MP4 beside the video's untouched samples (Sink Writer passthrough) and replaces the file, so cancelling or failing
  leaves the video as it was.
