# Milestone 6

Work in progress on several branches. This file collects each part's notes as it lands.

## Wallpaper types

Branch `m6/wallpaper-types` (from `next/windows` 10bcb62). Settings › Wallpaper now offers every one of the Mac's eight
wallpaper types, with the adjustments, legibility and the Covers page. Details: docs/APP-SHELL.md, "Wallpaper types
(milestone 6)"; the transparency and media decisions: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md; the per-song choice:
docs/compositions/matching.md, "The wallpaper".

### What each type does now

| Type | State in this build |
|---|---|
| Match the music (`auto`, now the default) | Picks a GPU effect or line pattern per song (`WallpaperMatch`, remembered per track for the session). Until those layers land it draws the album colours. |
| GPU effects (`shader`), Line patterns (`pattern`) | Menus ("Match the music" or a pinned style) filled from the effects/patterns agent's catalogue; album colours until then. |
| Visualisers (`visualizer`) | Selectable; a placeholder section until the visualiser agent's hook fills it; album colours meanwhile. |
| Your wallpaper (`desktop`) | **Done.** See-through surface: Windows' wallpaper or a wallpaper app shows behind the lyrics and text backing. |
| Imported image or video (`custom`) | **Done.** Picker, copy into `{data}\Wallpapers`, four placements, pictures (EXIF-upright, HEIC/AVIF where installed), videos (muted, looping, GPU-decoded, paused with the wallpaper, still under Reduce Motion). |
| Music video (`musicVideo`) | Disabled: "Arrives in a later build of milestone 6." |
| Colour only (`colourOnly`) | **Done**, with its "Album gradient" switch (off: the album colour field, Windows' old default, which old settings migrate to). |

Adjustments (Motion speed 0.2–2, Colour richness 0.4–1.8, Darkening 0–2, Pattern visibility 0–1, Reset Adjustments)
and Legibility (automatic or hand-set text backing) apply at once through `SceneSnapshot.Adjustments`, without
rebuilding layers and without jumps (the field's and the album gradient's clocks stay continuous). The tray flyout
shows every type as a tile.

### Transparency decision

Every wallpaper surface now presents a **premultiplied composition swap chain through DirectComposition** on its child
window under `Progman`; the frame is cleared transparent only while Your wallpaper is among the backgrounds. Checked
live on this PC (25H2, the 24H2 desktop): the system wallpaper showed through behind the demo's lyrics
(`docs/previews/wallpaper/live-desktop-pw-left-display.png`, PrintWindow; `…-thumb-…`, the DWM thumbnail). No
`IDesktopWallpaper` fallback was needed, and Lunalyr never reads or changes the user's wallpaper.

## GPU effects and line patterns

Branch `m6/gpu-effects`. This brings the Mac's **GPU effects** (`ShaderStyle`, 27) and **Line patterns**
(`BackgroundStyle`, 18) to Windows as background layers, coloured by the album as on the Mac.
- Layer ids: `shader.<id>` and `pattern.<id>`, with the Mac's raw values as ids.
- Provenance: [PROVENANCE.md](../PROVENANCE.md), "GPU effects and line patterns".
- Behaviour descriptions and Windows' titles: [GPU-EFFECTS.md](../GPU-EFFECTS.md).

### How it draws

- **Effects** (`Lunalyr.Render.Backgrounds.Gpu.GpuEffectLayer`):
  - Each frame runs one Direct3D 11 pixel shader as a full-screen triangle. It draws into a texture on the Direct3D
    device behind the surface's Direct2D context (`GpuCanvas`).
  - Direct2D stretches that texture over the surface, then draws the shared scrim, text backing and grain.
  - The canvas follows the Mac's pixel budget: 1.8 MP, 1.2 MP for Heavy effects, × 0.6 when economical.
  - The shared grade tints each effect with the album gradient, as the Mac's direct renderer does: five stops in
    OKLab, corner to corner. It also applies colour richness.
  - A palette change fades the stops over the surface fade. An effect change uses the engine's crossfade.
- **Patterns** (`LinePatternLayerFactory`):
  - The album colour field (`gradient`) is drawn first. The pattern is added over it as white light at
    0.85 × Pattern visibility, under the scrim and text backing.
  - Ten patterns are Direct2D painters for sparse strokes. Shapes that only turn are tessellated once, as geometry
    realizations.
  - Eight are pixel shaders at native resolution (Low Poly at half), for dense line fields.
  - A new song's seed rearranges the random details.
- **Shaders are compiled at build time** (`Backgrounds/Gpu/Shaders.targets`):
  - The Windows SDK's fxc compiles them for feature level 10_0 (`vs_4_0`/`ps_4_0`, `/WX`).
  - The bytecode is embedded as resources, so nothing compiles at run time and Native AOT has nothing to reflect over.
  - fxc is found through the Windows Kits root in the registry; override it with `-p:LunalyrFxc=`.
- **Clocks:**
  - The breathing runs on wall time × Motion speed, and the flow the shapes follow at 0.70 of that.
  - Reduce Motion runs both at 15%; the effects never stop, as on the Mac.
  - A change of speed or Reduce Motion never jumps the picture.
  - A layer's time base keeps the shader's floats small (`ShaderTimeBase`).
- **Power:**
  - Frame-rate caps and pauses are the engine's; a paused surface draws nothing.
  - Each effect has a measured tier (`GpuEffect.Heavy`, `WallpaperStyle.Heavy`). Heavy effects draw at the smaller
    pixel budget, and at 60% of it when economical. "Match the music" doesn't leave heavy effects out under power
    saving yet (see Wallpaper types, Open questions).
- **Adjustments:** Motion speed, Colour richness and Pattern visibility come from `SceneSnapshot.Adjustments` every
  frame, multiplied into the options as the colour field does (`SceneAdjust`). Darkening and the text backing come
  through the shared `WallpaperShadeDrawer`.
- **No allocations per frame:** tested for every effect and pattern on WARP. `GC.GetAllocatedBytesForCurrentThread`
  over 60 frames is 0.

### Cost

Measured offscreen with `tools/GpuEffectsPreview bench`: back-to-back frames, whole frame each. GPU time is D3D11
timestamps around the effect, stretch, scrim, backing and grain. CPU is the render-thread time to record and submit
the frame. Full tables:
- [Intel, 2560×1440](../measurements/gpu-effects/intel-2560x1440.md)
- [Intel, 3200×2000](../measurements/gpu-effects/intel-3200x2000.md): the laptop panel's size
- [RTX 5060, 2560×1440](../measurements/gpu-effects/rtx5060-2560x1440.md)
- the basis for the tiers: [Intel, every effect at 1.8 MP](../measurements/gpu-effects/intel-effects-at-standard-budget.md)

GPU ms per frame (the album colour field alone, for comparison: 0.53 / 0.84 / 0.12 ms):

| | Intel 2560×1440 | Intel 3200×2000 | RTX 5060 2560×1440 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light effects (15) | 0.86–1.10 | 1.19–1.42 | 0.16–0.20 |
| Medium effects (7: aurora, silk, helix, bubbles, strata, tidal, lightTrails) | 1.15–1.64 | 1.49–2.04 | 0.20–0.30 |
| Heavy effects at 1.2 MP (5: warp, nebula, voronoi, waves, clouds) | 1.46–1.90 | 1.82–2.22 | 0.25–0.30 |
| Painter patterns (bubbles, rain, stars, circuitry, tree, constellation, ripples) | 0.54–0.56 | 0.87–0.89 | 0.14–0.19 |
| Shader patterns, and Petals | 0.95–1.28 | 1.59–2.10 | 0.19–0.25 |
| Orbits / Spiral Arms | 2.19 / 2.54 | 3.63 / 4.19 | 0.55 / 0.62 |
| Render-thread CPU, worst layer | 0.32 | 0.48 | 0.38 |

What this measures: GPU busy time per frame. At 60 fps the frame has 16.7 ms, so the worst layer keeps the Intel GPU
busy about 15% of the time at 2560×1440 and 25% at 3200×2000 (Spiral Arms), and most layers well under 10%. It is not
a live 60 fps measurement on the desktop.

Two fixes came out of measuring:
- Orbits, Petals and Spiral Arms first drew turned path geometry with `DrawGeometry`, which tessellated it again every
  frame. Geometry realizations took their render-thread time from 2.7, 0.9 and 4.8 ms to 0.27, 0.12 and 0.34 ms.
- Low Poly finds its triangle among several per pixel. At native resolution it cost 5.5 ms on Intel at 3200×2000; it
  now draws at half resolution and is stretched (2.1 ms), its faint hairlines a little softer.

## Visualisers

The Mac's ten native visualiser scenes, reacting to the sound playing on the PC (branch `m6/visualisers`).

### What is built

- **Scenes** (`src/Lunalyr.Render/Visualisers/`): Silk Ribbons, Spectral Aurora, Spectrum Tunnel, Aurora Curtains,
  Fosfora Aurora, Wavefield, Wavefield Sphere, Star Trails, Colour Strata and Overprint. They are HLSL, compiled by the
  Windows SDK's `fxc` at build time and embedded (`Shaders/Shaders.targets`), so the AOT app carries no shader
  compiler. They target `ps_4_0`, so feature-level-10 devices draw them too.
  - One layer, `visualizer`, draws the chosen scene with Direct3D 11 into its own texture at the scene's pixel budget,
    and Direct2D lays it on the surface.
  - A scene change crossfades over 0.8 s. The scrim and text backing are the shared `WallpaperShadeDrawer`'s.
  - The Mac's three web scenes are out of scope. Stored values for them read as the native scene the Mac stands in
    with.
- **Audio analysis** (`src/Lunalyr.Core/Visualisers/`): the Mac's analyser (own FFT with vDSP's scaling), the sound
  types and their detector, the smoother, the shared scene clocks, Wavefield's modal bank, and Star Trails'
  simulation, sky and lyric word.
- **System audio** is WASAPI loopback of the default output device (`src/Lunalyr.Platform.Windows/Audio/`, through
  source-generated COM), for analysis only.
  - There is no render client: nothing is recorded or played, and no session is created that could make a sound.
  - **It is off until the user turns on "System audio".**
  - It follows default-device changes and device loss, reconnecting once the route settles.
  - It stops when nothing needs it. The Mac's gate applies: a visualiser on screen and something playing, with its
    hang times.
  - The output's peak meter says whether anything plays. Windows' loopback delivers no packets in silence, which is
    never reported as a fault.
  - `--no-playback` doesn't stop it (it only analyses). Under `--demo` a built-in synthetic signal feeds the same path
    and nothing is captured.
- **Safety**: the photosensitivity limiter runs on the GPU whenever the scene is calm, which is when Windows'
  Animation effects are off (the Mac's Reduce Motion).
  - Each frame is drawn into a 64 × 36 probe. A 1 × 1 pass measures its mean relative luminance and caps the rise at
    0.25 a second; falls are immediate. The full frame then multiplies by that gain, so the gain belongs to the frame
    it limits and the CPU never waits.
  - Calm also removes beat flashes (Punch reads 0), slows pattern changes, and reads the sound types with the slow,
    half-strength shapes.
  - Reduce Motion slows the scene clock to 15% without jumping.
  - A new layer for a scene already on screen keeps its brightness (ExposureMemory, keyed by scene within a host,
    read back once an event query says the copy is done); one coming on screen fades in from black.
- **Power**:
  - The render-scale ladder (fed by timestamp queries, 0.5–1 of the pixel budget) trims resolution when the GPU falls
    behind.
  - `BackgroundOptions.Economical` draws 60% of the pixels.
  - Power rules that fall back to line patterns or colour only replace the visualiser with the album colours, and
    pause System audio with the Mac's sentence.
- **Settings**, through the wallpaper-types hooks, each in its own file:
  - Visualiser: Scene, Colours (for Star Trails), and Beat punch with the calm caption.
  - Music response: System audio, Sensitivity, the status line, "System audio needs attention" with Reconnect, and
    Audio setup & diagnostics (live meters, the packet count, Copy Audio Diagnostics).
  - Keys: `visualizerStyle`, `audioReactiveEnabled`, `audioSensitivity`, `visualizerPunch`, `starTrailsColours`
    (docs/APP-SHELL.md).

### Open questions

- **Dim Flashing Lights**: Windows has no system setting for it, so only Animation effects (Reduce Motion) makes
  scenes calm. Should Lunalyr offer its own switch?
- **Output latency**: Windows reports none for loopback, and Bluetooth headphones' delay isn't visible to WASAPI, so
  the visuals don't wait; the Mac delays them up to 250 ms. A manual offset could cover Bluetooth.
- **Power fallback**: the shell doesn't yet replace backgrounds under power rules (the Mac's effective background). The
  visualiser layer does it for itself (album colours), and System audio pauses with the Mac's sentence.
- **Settings preview**: on the Mac it counts as a surface, keeping capture alive while the wallpapers are covered.
  Here only the wallpapers' surfaces count.
- The lyric ink's live audio input (`LyricInkDirector`'s "Live audio levels") isn't connected to System audio yet.
