## What works

- **Every lyric composition**, under the Mac's ids. Previews of each are in `docs/previews/compositions/`.

  | Family | Compositions | Mac renderer | Windows | Notes |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Editorial | Editorial, Spoken | (milestone 3) | | |
  | Scattered | Scattered, Constellation, Constellation + Editorial, Echo | `ScatteredLyricText` + `ConstellationLines` / `EchoBackdrop` | `LineSetLayer`, `ScatteredFamily.cs` | [scattered.md](../compositions/scattered.md); the Verso parts are re-implemented from behaviour |
  | Arrival | Cascade, Cutout | `SpokenTypographyView` | `SpokenLayer` with a `CompositionKind` | [cascade-cutout.md](../compositions/cascade-cutout.md) |
  | Stage | Hinge, Broadside, Portal, Mosaic, Torque, Fable | `StageLyricView` (no Verso lines) | `Compositions/Stage/` | [stage.md](../compositions/stage.md); faithful port |

  **"Match the music"** (`automatic`) uses the Mac's rotation, with order, counts, cursor and per-track memory. It
  chooses only among the layers the engine has registered. The Mac's `TrackMood` comes from Verso, so it is replaced
  by Windows' own `TrackCharacter` ([matching.md](../compositions/matching.md)).
- **Every reveal style:** Soft focus, Float up, Pop, Orbit, Glide, Unfold, Cutout · Fold, Cutout · Slide,
  Cutout · Highlight, Satin Ribbon, Split Ink and Custom mix. "Match the style" resolves per composition as on the
  Mac; Cutout's own entrance is Cutout · Fold.
- **Readings per word** (`SceneSnapshot.WordReadings`, `LineReadings`). Covers Japanese (bundled dictionary),
  Mandarin, Korean and saved Cantonese, shown below the words or instead of them.
  - Every layout reads each word, fragment or stage run: the Stage layouts pair readings by the runs they draw.
  - "Below" readings fill in syllable by syllable with their words in every layout (see "Finished after Test
    Build 4").
- **Credits** (ported from `CreditsLayer.swift`): "Title – Artist", with the artist on highlight tiles, then any
  production credits.
- **Instrumental-break dots**, with the lit dot's ring and progress after the last line.
- **Highlight colours:** complement, album or custom.
- **Fixes**
  - The previous song's cover is checked again before its colours are kept.
  - A held word no longer swells into its neighbour (the "neonRAIN" gap).
  - Energy saver wording in the Test Build 3 README.
  - MIT and Windows App SDK notices added to THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.
  - A race in the LRCLIB-agreement test fixed.

## Verified

- `dotnet test Lunalyr.slnx`: **1,458 tests** (1,457 pass, 1 opt-in skip), green on repeated full runs after each
  merge. 0 build warnings.
- **Mac tests ported with the Mac's inputs:** the stage layouts (29), Fable style, rolling stack, highlight timing,
  credits, Match the music (10), reading splits, and the entrance clearance for all ten entrances.
- **Offscreen renders** of every composition × fixture, plus every entrance in every whole-line composition, all
  looked at. Frames allocate nothing once a line is set; Spoken, Cascade and Cutout allocate only when a word joins or
  leaves the window.
- **Render-thread cost per frame** (RTX 5060, 2560×1440): 0.03–0.22 ms for every composition, except Portal at about
  0.75 ms. Setting a line takes 3–15 ms, done up to 1.5 s ahead.
- **AOT build:** published clean. A smoke run of Test Build 4 (`--demo --seconds 8`, automatic + Satin Ribbon)
  exited 0 with nothing at warning or above; "Match the music" chose Hinge for the demo song.

## Finished after Test Build 4

- **Reduce Motion** is in the scene (`SceneSnapshot.ReduceMotion`), filled from Windows' "Animation effects" (read
  only, followed while the app runs) and honoured by every composition, the highlight tiles, credits, backing vocals,
  interlude dots, the lyric colours and both album backgrounds, as on the Mac. See the compositions README.
- **Spoken and Cascade highlight tiles** lie behind the whole unit (word and reading), 9 × 3 pt past it, and tint the
  reading; a word missing from the window's line ends releases with the line on stage.
- **Stage layouts** pick a chosen entrance per run (Torque and Fable, as the Mac's `chosenEntrance`), and a line-timed
  line on a stage per phrase.
- **Readings fill in syllable by syllable** (the Mac's 76130ae, 24b8b2e, b46b130, 2c91c86, 970ae65). "Below" readings
  show each token's share of its word's reading as that token is sung ("omo", then "omoi", then "omoida" under 思い出)
  in Editorial, the Scattered family, Spoken, Cascade, Cutout and the six stage layouts. The Stage layouts pair
  word-by-word readings by their own runs (`LyricReadingPairs.Parts`), so each run carries only its own reading, and a
  run's reading is its parts' shares written together (思い出 reads "omoida", not "omo i da"). Reduce Motion shows a
  share at its onset. The reading is baked once and keeps its room as it fills in; frames still allocate nothing.
  Previews: `docs/previews/readings/`.
- **Lookup test:** the Lyrimuse source with no folder says "Choose the Lyrimuse lyrics folder" at once without being
  run, so `EnglishSongLrclibWordTimingBeatsLineTimedSources` asserts it exactly again.
- **Japanese songs under English titles** (the Mac's `next/localized-titles`, branch `m4/localized-titles`): language
  versions ("English Version", "Eng ver.", "(English)", "英語ver.") are version tags; lyrics in another script than the
  song's rank as another version ("lyrics in another language" in the source list); a song whose English title finds
  nothing usable is searched again under its Japanese title from Apple's catalogue ("found under the original
  title"). New setting `artistAliases` ("Search under other names", default on; no window until milestone 5). The
  player's genre now reaches the lookup. Cache policy v15. 15 Mac tests ported with the Mac's inputs, plus recorded
  iTunes Search replies. Details and the open "Idol" question: [LYRIC-MATCHING-NOTES.md](../LYRIC-MATCHING-NOTES.md).
