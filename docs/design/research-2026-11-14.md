# Redesign research, 14 Nov 2026: Settings and the menu-bar panel

What this is: the evidence for a full redesign of Lunalyr's Settings window and menu-bar panel, then three
directions derived from it. No direction was chosen before the research; each one below cites the findings
it comes from.

Evidence lives outside the repo, in `work/ui-redesign-research/`:
- `notes/apple.md`: Apple's HIG (Liquid Glass, settings, sidebars, menu-bar extras), System Settings, Control Center, Apple Music, and Apple Design Award winners 2022–2026 (fact-checked).
- `notes/music-media.md`: Sleeve 3, Apple Music, Spotify, Plexamp, LyricsX, Lyric Fever, Albums, Marvis Pro, Tuneful.
- `notes/pro-indie-mac.md`: Raycast, Linear, iA Writer, Things 3, CleanShot X, Nova, Bear 2, Superhuman, Sketch, Arc.
- `notes/menu-bar.md`: SoundSource 6, iStat Menus 7, Stats, Itsycal, Dato, Fantastical, Ice, Bartender, One Switch.
- `notes/windows-web.md`: Windows 11 Settings and Fluent, Material 3 dynamic colour, Linear, Vercel/Geist, Stripe, Polaris, GitHub (counter-example), Teenage Engineering OP-1 field, Nothing OS.
- `notes/writeups-showcases.md`: HIG, 37signals, NN/g, the "Mac-assed Mac app" writers, Rogue Amoeba, Linear and Raycast posts, critiques of System Settings, Rauno Freiberg, Emil Kowalski, Sebastiaan de With, WWDC18.
- `notes/lunalyr-audit.md`: Lunalyr today (Test Build 92), with a control inventory.
- `img/`: 64 screenshots from the sources (list and URLs in `img/SOURCES.md`) and 10 of Lunalyr today (`img/lunalyr/`).

Every claim in the notes carries a URL; unverified points are marked there.

## Method and skills

- **refero-design** (lead): research before design; "reference locks" (the sharp traits that make a
  reference itself) rather than averages; the anti-averaging rule when references conflict; the
  `anti-ai-slop.md`, `color.md`, `typography.md` and `craft-details.md` craft notes. It shaped the
  card test (cards only where the container is the interaction), the one-accent rule and the
  perceptual-colour rule below. The live Refero MCP was not connected; its offline workflow was used.
- **macos-design-guidelines**: the Settings scene on ⌘,, popovers for transient options, Esc and
  Return, semantic colours, Increase Contrast and Bold Text; the menu-bar-extra rules.
- **ui-ux-pro-max**: its database matched the music-streaming product row and the contrast, reduced-motion,
  breadcrumb-depth and preserve-screen-state guidelines; it had nothing on menu-bar panels or Mac settings.
- **awesome-design-md**: real token sets (Apple, Spotify, Linear, Raycast, Vercel, Stripe) used to
  compare colour roles and type ladders. They describe the brands' web pages, not their apps.
- **design:design-critique / research-synthesis**: the audit's structure and the clustering below.

## Findings (clustered across all areas)

**F1. The frame stays neutral; colour comes from content.** Spotify (one functional green), Marvis, Apple
Music full screen, Sleeve, Linear ("never decoratively"), SoundSource 5 (moved from green everywhere to
greys), Windows Mica (goes neutral when inactive), Tide Guide (colour in the sky, not the chrome). Apple:
"colour lives in content, not chrome". [music-media §Patterns 1; pro-indie-mac 7; menu-bar 9; windows-web 6; apple 2]

**F2. Colour taken from content is converted into roles in a perceptual space, clamped, and the user
keeps a say.** Material (HCT tones, contrast by tone distance), Stripe (CIELAB, five levels guarantee
4.5:1), Linear (theme = base + accent + contrast in LCH), Plexamp (four corner colours, because cover text
ruins blur; Flat / Smooth / UltraBlur and "follow album" as a policy). The recurring failure is raw,
unclamped saturation (Spotify and Plexamp legibility complaints). [windows-web 1–2; music-media 2]

**F3. Fewer, curated choices beat open pickers.** iA Writer: 3 fonts, 3 line lengths. Linear: a theme is
3 inputs. Bear: named theme packs. Sleeve: 7 shareable themes. Plexamp: 4 themes × 3 backgrounds.
HIG: "Minimize the number of settings". 37signals: preferences defer decisions. Tension: Sleeve,
Marvis and iStat thrive on depth, and strong defaults bring requests for control. [pro-indie-mac 5;
music-media 4; writeups 1; apple 3]

**F4. Two levels of disclosure, no more.** NN/g; Fluent (one expander level); Linear (summary, then
detail); SoundSource's ladder (row, disclosure, popover, own window); Halide (automatic, then manual tools
on demand). The failure is the catch-all "General" pane and depth hidden behind ambiguous ⓘ buttons
(System Settings). [writeups 2; windows-web 4; menu-bar 4; apple 3]

**F5. Settings are edited on the thing itself, or next to it.** iStat Menus: hover the menu preview to
get a switch and a handle per section. Ice and Bartender: drag the real menu-bar icons. iA: the preview
is the document. Albums: settings next to what they affect; the Settings tab is nearly empty. Raycast:
"Configure Command" from search. Sleeve: a resizable real-time preview beside every setting. Bartender 6
shows the preview must be truthful. [menu-bar 6; pro-indie-mac 1, 8; music-media 3]

**F6. Split by scope before pages.** Linear (Account / Features / Administration / Teams), Stripe
(Personal / Account / Product), Vercel (team / project with identical tabs). GitHub's one destination per
feature is the failure. [windows-web 3]

**F7. Navigation scales with page count.** Toolbar tabs up to about 8 (Things, Bear, Sketch); a sidebar
past 6–10 (Raycast, Stats, Ice, iStat); search past about 10, which helps but does not create
findability (Nova). Apple's own System Settings is criticised for unlabelled groups and arbitrary order.
[pro-indie-mac 2–3; menu-bar 7; apple 4; writeups 8]

**F8. One current thing, context receding.** Apple Music lyrics (active line full strength, the rest
dimmed), Flighty (one line per flight), Mela's cooking mode, LyricsX and Apple (a second script under
each line). [apple 6; music-media 6]

**F9. The menu-bar panel is narrow, sized to content, with controls at its edges and overflow pushed
out in steps.** Stats 264 pt wide; height follows content and scrolls only past the screen; header and a
thin bottom row (Itsycal 22 pt); the gear is a menu with Settings in it; ⌘, works in the panel; pin or
drag to detach (SoundSource, Fantastical, Stats, Itsycal); every panel closes on Esc. [menu-bar 1–4, 10]

**F10. Material is rationed by how long a surface lives.** Fluent: opaque Mica for windows, Acrylic only
for transient menus. Apple: Liquid Glass on the control and navigation layer, never on content; critics
document legibility failures (Music, Photos, the transparent menu bar). Rogue Amoeba avoids glass for
legibility; Dato lets users turn translucency off. [windows-web 7; apple 1; menu-bar 9]

**F11. Crafted means restrained and specific.** De-nesting (SoundSource), no icon on every row (tonsky),
checkboxes over switches for dense lists (Johnson), aligned labels and dimmed navigation (Linear), no
motion on frequent or keyboard actions (Emil Kowalski, Rauno Freiberg), small type ramps with two weights
(Windows: Regular and Semibold). [writeups 5; windows-web 8]

**F12. Several surfaces of graded intensity, each optional.** Lyric Fever (menu-bar line, karaoke float,
full screen), Tuneful (notch, popover, mini player), Spotify (window + Miniplayer), LyricsX. The user
decides what stays visible (HIG: "Let people, not your app, decide"). [music-media 5; apple 5]

## Three directions, derived from the findings

The findings split along three real tensions in the evidence: **where you change things** (beside the
picture vs on it, F5), **how many things there are to change** (depth vs curation, F3), and **what the
window is organised around** (pages vs the song and scope, F6, F8). Each direction resolves them
differently; none averages them (refero-design's anti-averaging rule).

### 1. "On the picture": edit Lunalyr where you see it
**Idea.** The live desktop preview is the window. You change the wallpaper and the lyrics by selecting
them on the preview; an inspector beside it shows only the settings of what is selected. Visual choices
(wallpaper types, the 14 layouts, entrances, typefaces) are pictures, not words.
**From:** F5 (iStat hover handles, Ice/Bartender real bars, iA's document as preview, Albums' contextual
settings, Sleeve's live preview, Raycast's Configure from the thing), F4 (inspector shows one level; detail
in a popover), F8 (the current line emphasised), audit hierarchy findings 1–3 and 5 (layouts as text, scope
told in captions). The sidebar stays for app settings only (F7: Words and App pages are few and
non-visual).
**Risk the evidence names:** the preview must be truthful (Bartender 6); a stand-in preview with nothing
playing must still be convincing.
**Menu-bar panel:** the sung line large (F8) with the cover; the 2–3 changes people make while listening
in one row; overflow to Settings in one step (F9).

### 2. "Looks": a few strong looks, the knobs one level down
**Idea.** Lunalyr ships a small set of named Looks (wallpaper type + layout + typeface + colour treatment
+ entrance, chosen together), shown large; you pick one, then adjust a handful of named qualities
(size, colour strength, motion). Everything else sits one disclosure down or in search. A quiet,
conventional Mac window around it: sidebar with labelled groups, system accent, no cards, rows on the
window background.
**From:** F3 (iA 3 fonts, Linear's 3-input theme, Sleeve's shareable themes, Plexamp presets, Bear packs,
HIG minimise settings, 37signals), F4 (two levels), F11 (de-nesting, no icon per row, Linear's aligned
uncarded rows, refero's card test), F1 and F10 (neutral, opaque, system accent: SoundSource "Follow System
Accent Color", HIG), F7 (sidebar + search). Audit: 230 controls, uneven pages, no presets.
**Risk the evidence names:** strong defaults bring requests for control (iA grew to about 150 options;
Bear widened to any font) and Lunalyr's users already tune a lot. Every current setting must stay
reachable.
**Menu-bar panel:** a compact, menu-like panel (HIG menu-bar extras; Stats/Itsycal width and edges) with
the Look switcher as its one rich control.

### 3. "Now playing": organised around the song, coloured by it
**Idea.** The song playing is the first scope: a "This song" area holds everything about the current
track (its lyrics source, timing offset, remembered style, find lyrics, the lyric card), apart from
"Every song" policy (the look, sources, players, app). The window and panel take a derived palette from
the cover (roles in a perceptual space, clamped to guaranteed contrast, with a user contrast setting and
an off switch), so Settings belongs to the music like the wallpaper and the desktop player already do.
Search and the keyboard reach any setting.
**From:** F6 (Linear, Stripe, Vercel scope splits; audit finding 8, per-song tools mixed with policy),
F2 (Material HCT roles, Stripe CIELAB levels, Linear's LCH base + accent + contrast, Plexamp's policy and
"follow album"; Lunalyr's own ArtworkPalette), F8 (current thing emphasised), F9 (panel: now playing first,
controls at edges, gear menu, detach and pin), F10 (opaque window, material only on transient surfaces),
pro-indie-mac 1 (Raycast settings from search, Superhuman's palette), F12 (surfaces under the user's control).
**Risk the evidence names:** unclamped colour fails (Spotify, Plexamp); colour must stay off controls
except one derived accent role (F1); with nothing playing the window needs a neutral state.

## What every direction must keep
Every current setting reachable; search; keyboard and VoiceOver as today; light and dark; Reduce Motion,
Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast; the slop list in `docs/DESIGN.md`; text 4.5:1; the menu-bar panel
fits the smallest screen in its busiest state (audit finding 9).
